📽️ Video Script (90 seconds)

[0:00-0:10] THE PROBLEM
"Millions of children struggle with homework. They turn to apps that just give the answer – no thinking, no learning. Parents are worried, teachers are frustrated. Something is missing: the joy of figuring things out on your own."

[0:10-0:30] THE SOLUTION
"Enter Homework Guide. An AI-powered smart tutor that never gives direct answers. Instead, it gives step-by-step hints – guiding the child to discover the solution themselves. We don't just teach math; we teach how to think."

[0:30-0:55] HOW IT WORKS
"Child snaps a photo of any question – math, science, language. Our Gemini AI reads it and generates progressive hints. The child types an answer; if wrong, another hint appears, and so on until they reach the correct solution. Then a cheerful cartoon character congratulates them."

[0:55-1:15] EXPECTED RESULTS
"We see improved reasoning skills, boosted confidence, and less homework anxiety. Early tests show children become more persistent and curious. This is not just an app – it's a learning companion."

[1:15-1:30] CLOSING & ASK
"We're raising $100k to scale our AI, add more languages, and partner with schools. Join us in transforming homework from a chore into an exciting challenge. Invest in the future of learning. www.homework-guide.com"
